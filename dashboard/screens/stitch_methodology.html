TECHNICAL METHODOLOGY

How the Model Works

A hybrid ensemble architecture combining gradient-boosted classification, survival analysis, and explainable AI — evaluated transparently against real performance benchmarks.

Risk Classification

A stacked ensemble of four gradient-boosted and tree-based models — XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, and ExtraTrees — each trained independently, with their outputs combined by a logistic regression meta-learner. Predictions are calibrated using Platt scaling to ensure probability outputs are reliable, not just well-ranked.

Timeline & Survival Prediction

Explainable AI

Every prediction is accompanied by a transparent explanation, not a black-box score. Meta-learner-weighted TreeSHAP attributions decompose each risk prediction into individual feature contributions, showing exactly which factors — terrain type, compensation disputes, clearance delays — pushed a project's risk score up or down, and by how much.
